// 04 · the method

read/ ship/ propose/ repeat.

Selection isn't a lottery. It's a four-step protocol with a known success rate. Run the loop.

01

read the source

Pick three orgs. Read their CONTRIBUTING.md. Lurk on the dev mailing list. Get a feel for the codebase shape.

02

ship one tiny pr

A typo fix counts. The point: prove you can land code in their tree. We'll surface their good-first-issues for you.

03

draft the proposal

Use templates from accepted students. Cut your scope by 40%. Run it past a mentor before submitting.

04

iterate publicly

Stay active during selection. Comment on PRs. Reply on the mailing list. The org is watching.

your year/ in commits.

Every solved problem, every PR, every proposal draft is a line in your commit log. Profile pages are reverse-chronological — recruiters and mentors see what you've shipped, not what you claim.
